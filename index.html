About

A build partner that stays through deployment.

We pair blockchain engineers, product designers and AI practitioners in one team, so strategy, design and engineering decisions get made together and nothing is lost in a handover.

Mission

Dependable infrastructure for teams building on-chain.

We provide reliable, modern blockchain infrastructure and development services that let organisations move from an idea to a system their users can trust.

Vision

A more connected, transparent and decentralised future.

We want open networks to be as dependable and easy to use as the systems they replace, and we measure our work by how well it holds up in production.

Approach

A delivery loop you can see into.

Short iterations, working software at each stage, and a clear decision point before the next one starts.

  1. Discover

    We map goals, constraints and the assumptions most likely to sink the project.

  2. Architect

    System design, chain and model selection, and a build plan with costs attached.

  3. Prototype

    An early working slice, tested with real users and real data.

  4. Build and audit

    Production engineering with security review and testing running alongside.

  5. Launch

    Deployment to cloud or mainnet, with monitoring and a documented handover.

  6. Support

    We measure, refine and extend, or step back once your team can run it alone.

FAQ

Questions we hear first.

Anything else, ask us through the form below.

Do you help with strategy before development?

Yes. Most engagements start with a short discovery phase that ends in an architecture and a costed build plan. You can take that plan anywhere.

Can you combine blockchain and AI in one project?

Yes, and it is a growing share of our work. A typical example is an AI agent that reads on-chain data and acts on it within limits you set.

How long does a project take?

A proof of concept usually takes weeks. A production platform takes months and is delivered in iterations, so you see working software throughout.

Do you work with both startups and enterprises?

Both. We adjust team size and process to fit, from a founder with a whitepaper to an established business adding an on-chain product.

Can we engage you for a single piece of work?

Yes. A design audit, a contract build or a bridge integration can each be scoped as a standalone engagement.

How do we get started?

Send us a note through the contact form with a few lines about what you are building. We will come back to arrange a first call.

Ready to build?

Tell us what you are working on. We will tell you how we would approach it and what it would take.
